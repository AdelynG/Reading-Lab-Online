L

LOCATE

Locate interests. Locate challenges. Locate the path forward.

Students begin with high-interest nonfiction text connected to their curiosity. Through purposeful reading, we identify strengths, uncover challenging word patterns, and create an individualized path toward stronger reading.

A

ANALYZE

Analyze words. Analyze text. Analyze meaning.

Students become reading scientists as they investigate words, syllable patterns, phrases, and text structure. Using explicit instruction, phrase-cued reading, and annotation strategies, students learn how to unlock meaning.

B

BUILD

Build fluency. Build understanding. Build confidence.

Through repeated practice and meaningful texts, students strengthen accuracy, automaticity, expression, and comprehension–building skills they can transfer to any classroom.
