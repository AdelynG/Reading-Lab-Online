Reading fluency is the bridge to comprehension. Fluency, at the word, phrase, and sentence levels, is a skill that can be strengthened.

At The Reading Lab, students build confidence and independence through explicit, systematic reading instruction designed around their individual needs. Using research-based practices, engaging texts, and targeted routines, students learn strategies to decode, understand, and think deeply about what they read.

Small-group and individualized instruction for K-8 learners.
